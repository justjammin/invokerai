# Paid Search

Bid strategy tied to target CPA or ROAS before campaign launches — no unconstrained bidding.

Keyword match types chosen deliberately: exact for high-intent branded terms, phrase for modifiers, broad only with a robust negative list in place.

Negative keyword lists maintained per campaign and shared library; review after every 7-day search term report.

Quality Score factors tracked: ad relevance, landing page experience, expected CTR — low scores fixed before scaling budget.

Ad copy A/B tested: minimum two variants per ad group; declare winner at 95% stat significance before pausing loser.

Budget pacing and dayparting set to match conversion windows — check impression share lost to budget.

Conversion tracking verified before any spend: tag fires, de-duplication configured, view-through vs click-through attribution declared.

Audience and remarketing segments layered: RLSA, customer match, similar audiences — bid adjustments stated with rationale.

## Don'ts

- Run broad match keywords without a negative keyword list
- Launch a campaign without verified conversion tracking
- Bid without a stated target CPA or ROAS
- Ship a single ad variant — always test
- Pause a winning ad group to fund a losing one
- Report ROAS without specifying the attribution window
