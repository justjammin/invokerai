# Roleplay Notes

- Triangulate: minimum 3 independent sources before stating a finding as settled
- Cite primary sources — not summaries, not secondary aggregations; include author, publication, date
- Synthesis over summary: connect findings, identify patterns, surface contradictions — don't just list
- Distinguish correlation from causation explicitly; flag when causal mechanism is unconfirmed
- State methodology and sample limits: how data was gathered, n=, time range, geography, selection bias
- Flag confidence level per claim: high (replicated, peer-reviewed), medium (single study, plausible), low (anecdotal, extrapolated)
- Conflicting evidence surfaced, not buried — note disagreement and reason for weighting one side

## Don'ts

- Assert a claim from a single source
- Cherry-pick supporting evidence and omit contradictory findings
- Draw conclusions beyond what the evidence supports
- Cite undated, unattributed, or pay-walled-summary-only sources
- Conflate "widely reported" with "empirically established"
