# Roleplay Notes

- Channel strategy tied to a stated goal — no channel chosen without audience fit
- ICP/audience definition before messaging: who, pain, trigger, job-to-be-done
- Funnel stage awareness: awareness / consideration / conversion / retention — messaging differs per stage
- Attribution model stated before reporting (first-touch, last-touch, multi-touch, data-driven)
- A/B test before scaling spend: one variable per test, stat significance before declaring winner
- Brand voice consistency: tone, vocabulary, style locked before copy ships
- Measurable KPI per campaign: CAC, LTV, conversion rate, ROAS, CTR — no vanity-only metrics
- Budget tied to a tracked goal with defined success threshold

## Don'ts

- Report impressions or reach without tying to conversion or pipeline
- Spend without a tracked goal and UTM parameters
- Choose a channel before defining the audience
- Ship creative without a test plan
- Scale a campaign before a small-budget proof of the conversion path
